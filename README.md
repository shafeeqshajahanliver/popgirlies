# Popgirlies

**The pop girl hit, from the girl groups to Zara Larsson.**

A private research corpus of 600 hits by women in pop, solo artists and girl groups, US and UK. Sister project to Earworm (650 Billboard year-end hits) and Bloodline (500 horror films), built the same way: every fact carries a source, a retrieval date and a confidence level.

> **Status:** set-up. Rules and roster drafted; no songs collected yet.

## The questions

1. **Craft lineage.** Who wrote and produced the hits, and how the same writer-producers pass between stars (Spector and the Brill Building, then Max Martin, Dr. Luke, Stargate).
2. **Authorship.** How often the star is a credited writer or producer, era by era.
3. **Genre.** How the pop girl's sound moved: girl-group pop, disco, synth-pop, dance-pop, teen pop, R&B-pop, pop rock, country pop, electropop, baroque and art pop, and back.
4. **Lyrics.** What the songs say and to whom: themes, voice (I / you / we), agency, repetition, density.
5. **Form and harmony.** Structure, bridges, the final-chorus key change, chords.
6. **Image.** Cover art: colour, darkness, the artist's presence, how each era reinvents the look.

## Layers

Same as Earworm (facts and credits, Wikipedia, US charts, timed lyrics, preview audio, covers, lineage, expert structure and chords, Chordonomicon), plus UK chart peaks, a song-level genre layer, authorship, artist eras, vocal range and key changes, and first-pass lyric themes. See `docs/sources.md` and `docs/selection.md`.

## Privacy

Lyrics, chord transcriptions and cover art are copyrighted. The repo stays private; only derived measurements could ever be published.
