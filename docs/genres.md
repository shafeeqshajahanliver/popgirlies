# Genre taxonomy (draft)

Each song keeps its raw genre labels (Wikipedia infobox, MusicBrainz tags, Wikidata P136) and gets one or more normalised styles below. A song qualifies when at least one style is a pop style. Confidence: `sourced` for infobox and Wikidata labels, `crowd` for MusicBrainz tags.

| Family | Normalised styles (examples of raw labels) |
|---|---|
| Girl-group and early pop | girl group, doo-wop pop, Brill Building pop, Motown / soul-pop, bubblegum |
| Disco and dance | disco, Hi-NRG, Eurodance, dance-pop, house-pop, EDM-pop, Eurodisco |
| Synth and electronic | synth-pop, electropop, electroclash, hyperpop, bitpop |
| Teen and bubblegum | teen pop, bubblegum pop |
| R&B-pop | R&B-pop, new jack swing, contemporary R&B (pop-crossover only), hip-hop-pop |
| Rock-pop | pop rock, power pop, pop-punk, new wave |
| Country-pop | country pop, pop country |
| Singer-songwriter and ballad | soft rock, adult contemporary, pop ballad, folk-pop |
| Art and baroque | art pop, baroque pop, chamber pop, dream pop, indie pop |
| Latin and global | Latin pop, reggaeton-pop, K-pop, Europop |
| Other pop | pop (unqualified), alt-pop, bedroom pop, trap-pop |

Analysis tracks each family's share by year, the artists who move between families across their careers, and which families carry which lyric themes and production traits (tempo, loudness, brightness).
