# Queer lens (planned, later phase)

How pop girls and queer culture intersect: who made the songs, what the songs say, and who claimed them. Four separate layers, never merged into one label.

| Layer | What it records | Source and confidence | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Artist identity | An artist's **own public statement** of LGBTQ+ identity, with the date it was first made public | Sourced: the artist's own words in an interview or post, as cited by Wikipedia or a reliable outlet | Never inferred from lyrics, image, rumour or Wikidata alone. Absence is recorded as "not publicly stated", never as straight. The date matters: identity at the time of the song is kept separate from identity today. Group members are recorded individually. |
| 2. Collaborators | Publicly out writers, producers, video directors and choreographers on each song | Same rule as layer 1, per person | Shows queer authorship behind the scenes, not just on stage. |
| 3. Lyrics | Songs with explicit same-sex desire, gender play or queer themes ("I Kissed a Girl", "Born This Way", "Good Luck, Babe!") | First pass from lyrics and the song's article, quoted; Shaf reviews | Tags describe the text, never the singer. Coded or ambiguous readings are marked as interpretation and kept apart from explicit content. |
| 4. Reception | Songs claimed by queer audiences: Pride and club anthems, ballroom and drag culture references ("Vogue"), gay-icon status, LGBTQ+ awards | Sourced from the song's or artist's Wikipedia legacy and reception sections, and named awards (e.g. GLAAD) | Records what sources say about the audience, not a judgement of the song. |

Questions this enables: how the share of openly queer stars changed by generation; whether queer themes moved from coded to explicit; how often queer collaborators shaped straight-presenting stars' hits; which songs became anthems without any queer content.

All of this is private research. Identity data about living people is handled conservatively: only what they themselves made public, with the source kept beside it.
